What it is

JUNG ONE: The Garden is a hybrid card-and-online conversation system for adult children and ageing parents.

It helps families open questions about care, distance, memory, responsibility, and personal boundaries — before urgent decisions turn them into crisis.

How it works
  1. Choose a question
  2. Attach a listening signal
  3. Invite your family
  4. Talk
  5. Keep one chosen trace
  6. Return to the garden
Online  ·  Offline
Online, a question becomes an invitation.
Offline, a card conversation becomes a trace.
Both return to the same garden.
